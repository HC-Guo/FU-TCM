FU-TCM training

Training moves from domain adaptation to structured case reasoning and Bianzheng-Grounded Policy Optimization.

1

Full-parameter domain SFT

Adapt the base vision-language models with TCM text and image question-answer data. The public 9B path uses DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, bf16, and a 4,096-token context.

llamafactory_qwen35/qwen35_9b_full_sft_ds3.yaml

2

Cold-start SFT

Teach the required structured case format and the path from four-examination evidence through intermediate bianzheng judgments to the final syndrome.

3

BGPO reasoning alignment

Apply verifiable rewards for format validity, syndrome consistency, and fidelity across 30 intermediate diagnostic fields.

reward_functions.py::compute_score

4

Evaluation

Run text and vision benchmarks with Transformers or vLLM and generate blinded materials for clinician review.

eval_qwen35.py · eval_qwen35_vllm.py
