FU-TCM model overview

FU-TCM is a multimodal large-language-model family that makes the path from four-examination evidence through intermediate bianzheng judgments to the final syndrome explicit and reviewable.

Model configuration

Model familyFU-TCM-9B and FU-TCM-27B
Base modelsQwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-27B
ModalitiesText and image
Supervised alignmentFull-parameter SFT
Reasoning alignmentBianzheng-Grounded Policy Optimization
Public training pathQwen3.5-9B · bf16 · DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 · LLaMA-Factory · verl

Core capabilities

TCM knowledge

Answers questions grounded in classical works, formulas, materia medica, theory, textbooks, and clinical knowledge.

Multimodal understanding

Connects textual context with tongue, face, herb, and other medical images.

Structured reasoning

Represents the four examinations, eight principles, organ systems, qi-blood-fluid states, and pathogenic factors.

Inspectable outputs

Exposes 30 intermediate fields linking clinical evidence to the final syndrome.

BGPO alignment objective

FormatRequired response structure
SyndromePrediction consistency
FieldsIntermediate fidelity

These verifiable rewards evaluate both the diagnostic path and the final result rather than surface fluency alone.

Evaluation summary

FU-TCM-27B achieved an 81.01% macro-average across six benchmarks and ranked first on all six tasks. Across eight models, mean intermediate-field accuracy correlated with final syndrome-choice accuracy at Pearson r = 0.89.
